About Oppora

We build the bridge between talent and opportunity

Oppora exists because brilliant people keep missing life-changing deadlines they never knew existed. We fix discovery, explanation and preparation — so effort meets opportunity.

Our mission

Africa does not have an opportunity shortage. It has an opportunity-discovery problem. Scholarships close with thousands of unopened seats. Grants expire unknown. Internships fill through whisper networks.

Oppora aggregates, verifies and explains opportunities — then matches them to a living profile we call the Opportunity Passport. Every match shows its working. Every deadline gets intelligence. Every application gets preparation.

Policies, in plain language

Privacy

We collect only what matching needs: your Passport, saves and application states. We never sell data. In the prototype everything stays on your device; production adds encryption at rest, GDPR/NDPR rights and a one-click export.

Terms of service

Oppora is a discovery and preparation layer. Providers remain the source of truth for eligibility, deadlines and decisions. Misuse — scraping, fake reports, fee requests — terminates access.

Cookie policy

We use essential local storage for your session and Passport. No advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers. Analytics (production) are privacy-preserving and opt-outable.
